Who are we?

We are a passionate team dedicated to revolutionizing the way futsal is played, managed, and experienced. Based in Amarsingh Chowk, Pokhara, our goal is to create a digital bridge between futsal players, hosts, and organizers through an efficient and user-friendly platform. Founded in 2025, our Futsal Management System was built to simplify bookings, support event organization, and help futsal venues grow — all while promoting an active, sports-driven lifestyle in our community and beyond.

Why Choose Us?

At Futsal Management System (FMS), we believe in delivering more than just a booking platform — we offer a complete futsal experience. Our system is designed to make booking courts quick, simple, and hassle-free. With secure payment options, real-time booking updates, and a user-friendly interface, players and hosts alike can manage their games and events effortlessly. We are committed to innovation, reliability, and excellence, ensuring that every user — whether a passionate player or a venue owner — receives the best service possible. Choose FMS for a smarter, faster, and more connected way to experience futsal.
